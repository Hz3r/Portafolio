**Vibecoding** is programming with an AI assistant as your main copilot: you describe the intent, the model writes the code, you validate, tweak and iterate again.

## When it works well

- Prototypes and MVPs where speed matters more than perfection
- Well-scoped pieces: components, utilities, tests
- Exploring a new framework without drowning in tutorials

## When it does not

- Critical systems where every decision must be deliberate
- Code you do not understand: if you cannot read it, you cannot maintain it

> Vibecoding does not remove the developer's judgement; it amplifies it. The AI writes fast, but you decide what deserves to exist.
